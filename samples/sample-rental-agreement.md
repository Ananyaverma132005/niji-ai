# Residential Rental Agreement (Sample)

This is a fictional sample document for trying Niji AI. All names, addresses and amounts are made up.

## 1. Parties

This Rental Agreement is made on 1 July 2026 between Mr. Rakesh Sharma (the "Landlord") and Ms. Priya Das (the "Tenant") for the flat described below.

## 2. Property

Flat No. 304, Lotus Residency, Patia, Bhubaneswar, Odisha 751024. The flat is a furnished 2BHK with two ceiling fans in each room, a refrigerator, a washing machine, one double bed, one wardrobe and a modular kitchen. A full inventory signed by both parties is attached as Annexure A.

## 3. Term

The tenancy is for 11 months, starting 1 July 2026 and ending 31 May 2027. It may be renewed for a further 11 months by mutual written agreement at least 30 days before the end of the term.

## 4. Rent

The monthly rent is ₹18,000 (Rupees Eighteen Thousand only), payable in advance on or before the 5th day of each month by bank transfer to the Landlord's account. If rent is paid after the 10th of the month, a late fee of ₹100 per day will be charged until payment is received.

On renewal, the rent will increase by 5% per year.

## 5. Security Deposit

The Tenant has paid an interest-free security deposit of ₹54,000 (equal to three months' rent). The Landlord will return the deposit within 15 days after the Tenant hands over the keys, after deducting any unpaid rent, unpaid utility bills, and the cost of repairing damage beyond normal wear and tear. The Landlord must give the Tenant an itemised list of any deductions.

A deduction of up to ₹6,000 may be made for repainting the flat if the walls are marked, stained or drilled without permission.

## 6. Lock-in Period and Notice

There is a lock-in period of 6 months, from 1 July 2026 to 31 December 2026. If the Tenant leaves during the lock-in period, the Tenant must pay rent for the remaining months of the lock-in period, or the Landlord may deduct this amount from the security deposit.

After the lock-in period, either party may end this agreement by giving one month's written notice. The Landlord may end the agreement immediately if rent is unpaid for two consecutive months.

## 7. Utilities and Charges

The Tenant will pay electricity bills as per the separate meter, the water charges of ₹400 per month, internet and cooking gas. The society maintenance charge of ₹2,500 per month will be paid by the Landlord.

## 8. Repairs and Maintenance

Minor repairs costing up to ₹1,000 each, such as replacing bulbs, tap washers, switches and fuses, will be done and paid for by the Tenant.

Major repairs, including plumbing leaks inside walls, electrical wiring faults, structural problems and the repair of appliances provided by the Landlord (refrigerator and washing machine), will be paid for by the Landlord, unless the damage was caused by the Tenant's misuse. The Tenant must inform the Landlord of any major repair within 3 days of noticing it.

## 9. Use of the Property

The flat will be used only as a residence for the Tenant and her immediate family. The Tenant may not sublet the flat or any part of it, or run any business from it.

Pets are allowed only with the Landlord's prior written permission. Small pets such as cats or small dogs will usually be permitted; the Tenant will be responsible for any damage caused by the pet and for keeping common areas clean.

Guests may stay for up to 7 consecutive nights without informing the Landlord. Longer stays require the Landlord's consent. Smoking is not allowed inside the flat.

## 10. Alterations

The Tenant shall not make structural changes, drill walls for heavy fixtures, or repaint the flat without the Landlord's written consent. Removable fixtures such as curtain rods may be installed if the walls are restored when the Tenant leaves.

## 11. Inspection

The Landlord may inspect the flat once a month, at a reasonable time, after giving at least 24 hours' notice to the Tenant, except in an emergency such as a water leak or fire.

## 12. Vacating the Property

When the agreement ends, the Tenant will hand over the flat in the same condition as at the start, apart from normal wear and tear, along with all keys (two main-door keys and one mailbox key). Both parties will check the flat against the Annexure A inventory on the handover date.

## 13. Disputes

Any dispute will first be discussed between the parties in good faith. If it is not resolved within 30 days, it will be subject to the jurisdiction of the courts in Bhubaneswar.

Signed by the Landlord and the Tenant in the presence of two witnesses on 1 July 2026.
